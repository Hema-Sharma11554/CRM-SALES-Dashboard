🔍 Key Business Insights from This Dashboard

1.  Declining Trend in Q4: Opportunities Won dropped to 1,985 (-62 vs Prev) - needs pipeline push in Q1 2018
2.  Strong Win Rate: 60.3% Win % is healthy, but 36.8% Lost (2,473) indicates competitor or pricing issue
3.  Top Performer Gap: Darcel Schlecht (553) is 60% ahead of average (279) - replicate his sales playbook for training
4.  Bottom Performers: Wilbur Farran (79), Rosalina Dieter (110), Rosie Papadopoulos (121) need coaching
5.  Quarterly Growth: From 647 opps in Q1 to ~2,000 in Q2-Q4 shows 3x business scale-up
6.  Agent Consistency: Top 5 agents (Darcel, Vicki, Anna, Kary, Donn) contributed 1,836 opps (27% of total) - high dependency risk

💡 Recommendations

1.  Coaching Program: Pair bottom 5 agents with top 3 (Darcel, Vicki, Anna) for shadowing
2.  Win/Loss Analysis: Analyze 2,473 lost opps for reasons (price, product, timing)
3.  Pipeline Building: Q1 2017 was only 647 opps vs 2,000+ later - avoid Q1 dip in 2018 with early prospecting in Dec
4.  Incentive Structure: Reward consistency, not just Q4 spike - Darcel consistent across all quarters
5.  Regional Filter: Use `regional_office` and `manager` slicers (top right) to identify low-performing regions
